Platform Policy

Acceptable Use Policy

Guidelines for protecting the CurisData platform, its data, and the work of the people who rely on it.

This policy describes activities that are not permitted when using CurisData.

Users of CurisData agree not to:

  • Scrape platform data
  • Use automated bots or crawlers
  • Extract large datasets
  • Build competing analytics platforms
  • Attempt unauthorized access

Enforcement

Accounts violating these rules may be suspended or terminated.

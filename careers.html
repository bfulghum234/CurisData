Careers at CurisData

Help build a sharper view of healthcare opportunity.

We are looking for curious builders who can turn complex healthcare and market information into useful, decision-ready intelligence.

How We Work

Small team, meaningful data problems.

CurisData sits at the intersection of healthcare, data engineering, geospatial intelligence, and practical business decisions. We value people who enjoy figuring out messy data, improving repeatable processes, and explaining their work clearly.

If your experience does not match every line below but you can make a strong case for how you would help, we would like to hear from you.

What We Value

Sound judgment, careful data handling, initiative, and a willingness to learn quickly as the product and its data foundations evolve.

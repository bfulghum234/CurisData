Methodology

A transparent way to estimate specialty utilization.

CurisData analyzes individual-level healthcare survey records weighted to represent the U.S. civilian, noninstitutionalized population, then models how specialty use varies across the demographic profile of a defined market.

Population-based evidence

We use healthcare survey evidence that represents people who use specialty care and people who do not, creating a population denominator rather than relying only on provider records.

Local demographic application

Observed utilization relationships are applied to the age, sex, race and ethnicity, region, and income profile of the population in the market being evaluated.

Clear scope for planning

These estimates cover annual office-based and hospital outpatient physician visits across 25 specialties. They are modeled resident-generated utilization estimates, not provider-volume projections, inpatient or emergency-department demand, unmet-need measures, or guarantees about future behavior.
